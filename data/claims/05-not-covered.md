# Pinewright Mutual: What a sample policy does not cover

**Fictional sample document.** Pinewright Mutual does not exist. The exclusions below
are invented for a prototype and describe no real policy.

## Excluded in this sample
- Wear and tear, gradual deterioration, and lack of maintenance.
- Damage from insects, rodents or mould that developed over time.
- Flood and earthquake, which the sample describes as separate policies.
- Property of a business run from the unit.
- A vehicle and anything built into it.
- Intentional damage by the policyholder.
- Cash above the sublimit printed on the schedule.

## Why intake still records an excluded sounding loss
An exclusion written in a sample document is not a decision about a particular loss. A
caller describing something that sounds excluded is still recorded, because the facts
matter and a handler decides. This prototype never tells a caller their loss is
excluded, and never tells them it is covered.
