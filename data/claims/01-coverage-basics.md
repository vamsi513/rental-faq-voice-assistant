# Pinewright Mutual: Coverage basics

**Fictional sample document.** Pinewright Mutual does not exist. Every figure and policy
statement below is invented for a prototype.

## What a Pinewright renters policy is said to include
A sample Pinewright renters policy describes three parts:

- **Personal property.** Belongings inside the rented unit, up to the limit printed on
  the policy schedule.
- **Personal liability.** Claims by other people for bodily injury or property damage
  the policyholder is responsible for.
- **Loss of use.** Reasonable additional living costs if the unit becomes uninhabitable
  after a covered loss.

## Policy numbers
A Pinewright policy number in this prototype has the form `PW-` followed by eight
digits, for example `PW-40281773`. Anything else is not a policy number this intake
flow will accept.

## What this document does not do
It does not say whether any particular loss is covered. Coverage depends on the policy
schedule, the endorsements on it, and the facts of the loss. A coverage decision is made
by a claims handler, never by this prototype.
