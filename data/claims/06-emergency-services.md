# Pinewright Mutual: When to call emergency services

**Fictional sample document.** Pinewright Mutual does not exist. The instructions below
are invented for a prototype.

## Call emergency services first
The sample instructions say to call emergency services on **911** before reporting a
loss, when:

- anyone is injured, however slightly;
- there is a fire, smoke, or a smell of gas;
- there is a medical emergency;
- there has been a break in and the intruder may still be present;
- a structure looks unsafe to enter or a live electrical hazard is present.

## Then the 24 hour line
Once anyone in danger is safe and emergency services have been called, the sample
instructions say to call the 24 hour line on **(555) 0144** so urgent mitigation can
start. Reporting the loss can wait.

## Why automated intake stops
Automated intake stops when an injury or an emergency is reported. It hands off to a
person. A script collecting fields is the wrong thing in front of someone who needs
help, and injury reports carry liability questions that need a handler.
