# Pinewright Mutual: The claim process

**Fictional sample document.** Pinewright Mutual does not exist. The steps below are
invented for a prototype.

## Reporting a loss
The sample process has four stages:

1. **Report.** Policy number, the date of loss, what kind of loss it was, a short
   description, and whether anyone was injured.
2. **Acknowledge.** A claim number is issued and a handler is assigned.
3. **Review.** The handler may ask for photographs, receipts, or a police or fire
   report.
4. **Decision.** The handler decides coverage and the amount payable, applies the
   deductible, and writes to the policyholder.

## What intake can and cannot do
Intake records what the caller reports. It does not decide coverage, does not estimate a
payment, and does not promise that a loss will be paid. Those are the handler's.

## Injuries
If anyone was injured, the sample process routes the report to a person rather than
through automated intake, so that medical and liability questions are handled by someone
who can ask follow up questions.
