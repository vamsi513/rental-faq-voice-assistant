# Pinewright Mutual: Claim hours

**Fictional sample document.** Pinewright Mutual does not exist. Every hour and number
below is invented for a prototype.

## Reporting hours
| | |
|---|---|
| Automated intake | 24 hours a day |
| Claims handlers | Monday to Friday, 8am to 7pm; Saturday 9am to 1pm |
| Closed | Sundays and public holidays |

## After hours
A loss reported outside handler hours is recorded and queued. A handler makes contact on
the next working day. Recording a loss is not the same as a handler reviewing it.

## The emergency line
Situations that cannot wait for the next working day, in this sample, use the 24 hour
line on **(555) 0144**. That line is for urgent loss mitigation, not for coverage
questions.

## Emergency services come first
The emergency line is not a substitute for emergency services. If anyone is hurt or in
danger, the sample instructions say to call emergency services first.
