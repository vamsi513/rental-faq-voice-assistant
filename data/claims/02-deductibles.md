# Pinewright Mutual: Deductibles

**Fictional sample document.** Pinewright Mutual does not exist. Every figure below is
invented for a prototype.

## How a deductible works in this sample
The deductible is the amount subtracted from a covered loss before payment. A sample
Pinewright renters policy offers three:

| Deductible | Effect described in the sample schedule |
|---|---|
| $500 | Standard |
| $1,000 | Lower premium |
| $2,500 | Lowest premium |

## Per loss, not per year
Each separate loss carries its own deductible in this sample. Two unrelated losses in
one policy year mean two deductibles.

## Where the real number lives
The deductible that applies to a specific policy is printed on that policy's schedule.
This prototype does not read policy schedules and cannot look one up. It cannot tell a
caller what their deductible is.
