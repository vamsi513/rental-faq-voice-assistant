# Maple Court Apartments: Lease Terms

## Standard lease length
The standard lease is 12 months. A 6-month lease is available at $150 per month above
the published rate for that floor plan. Month-to-month is available only after a
completed 12-month lease, at $300 per month above the published rate.

## Renewal
Renewal offers are sent 90 days before the lease end date. The resident must respond at
least 60 days before the end date. A lease that is not renewed and not ended with proper
notice converts to month-to-month at the month-to-month rate.

## Notice to vacate
Written notice to vacate is required at least 60 days before the intended move-out date.
Notice must be submitted in writing through the resident portal or delivered to the
leasing office.

## Breaking a lease early
Early termination requires 60 days written notice plus a termination fee of two months'
rent. The security deposit is handled separately and is not applied to the termination
fee.

Residents with active military orders may terminate under the Servicemembers Civil
Relief Act with a copy of the orders; the termination fee does not apply in that case.

## Occupancy
Occupancy is limited to 2 people per bedroom plus 1. A studio allows up to 2 occupants.
Guests staying longer than 14 consecutive days must be added to the lease.

## Subletting
Subletting and short-term rental listings are not permitted.

## Quiet hours
Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM daily.

## Smoking
Maple Court is entirely smoke-free, including inside units, on balconies, and in all
common areas. This includes vaping. A smoking violation carries a $250 charge.
