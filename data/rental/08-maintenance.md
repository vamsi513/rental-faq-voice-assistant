# Maple Court Apartments — Maintenance

## Routine maintenance requests
Routine requests are submitted through the resident portal, by phone to (555) 0100
during office hours, or in person at the leasing office. A request should include the
unit number, a description of the problem, and whether there is a pet in the unit.

Target response times, measured in business days:
- Urgent but not an emergency, such as a refrigerator not cooling or a single
  non-functioning toilet in a two-bath unit: next business day.
- Routine, such as a dripping faucet, a loose cabinet handle, or a burned-out common
  fixture: 3 business days.
- Cosmetic, such as paint touch-up or a scuffed baseboard: scheduled as workload allows.

These are targets, not guarantees.

## Entry for maintenance
Maintenance staff may enter with at least 24 hours notice, except in an emergency, when
entry may occur without notice. Residents may request to be present; this must be noted
on the request and may extend the schedule.

If a pet is in the unit, note it on the request. Technicians will not enter a unit with
an unsecured animal.

## Emergency maintenance
For an emergency, call the 24-hour emergency line at (555) 0199 and press 1. Do not use
the online queue, which is reviewed only during office hours.

An emergency is: fire, a gas odor, flooding or an active water leak, no heat when the
outside temperature is below 55 degrees Fahrenheit, a total loss of power inside the
unit, a broken exterior door or window lock, or a sewage backup.

For fire, gas odor, or any immediate danger to a person, call 911 first, then the
emergency line.

## Charges for repairs
Normal wear and tear is repaired at no cost to the resident. Charges apply for damage
caused by the resident, a guest or a pet. Published examples:
- Garbage disposal jam caused by improper use: $75.
- Drain clog caused by wipes, grease or foreign objects: $125.
- Lockout after office hours: $50.
- Broken window or door glass: billed at actual cost.
- Missed appointment where the resident requested to be present: $40.

## Filters and resident upkeep
Air filters are replaced quarterly by maintenance at no charge. Residents are
responsible for replacing their own light bulbs in unit fixtures and batteries in smoke
detectors, and for keeping drains free of grease and foreign objects.
