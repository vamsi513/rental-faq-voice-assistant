# Maple Court Apartments: Published Rent and Fees

## Monthly rent by floor plan
These are published starting rates for a 12-month lease. Actual pricing for a specific
unit is quoted by the leasing office.

- Studio, about 480 square feet: $1,150 per month.
- One bedroom, about 650 square feet: $1,375 per month.
- Two bedroom, two bath, about 925 square feet: $1,790 per month.

## Security deposit
The standard security deposit is one month's rent. It is refundable after move-out,
less any deductions for damage beyond normal wear, unpaid charges, or cleaning beyond
standard turnover.

## Recurring monthly charges
Added to rent each month:
- Water, sewer and trash: $65 flat per unit.
- Pet rent, if applicable: $35 per pet per month.
- Reserved parking, if applicable: $40 per space per month.

Electricity and internet are billed directly by the provider and are the resident's
responsibility. They are not collected by Maple Court.

## One-time and situational fees
- Application fee: $50 per adult, non-refundable.
- Holding deposit: $300, applied to first month's rent if the lease is signed.
- Pet fee: $250 per pet, one time, non-refundable.
- Late rent fee: $75, charged once rent is more than 5 days late.
- Returned payment fee: $35.
- Replacement key or fob: $25.
- Lost parking decal: $15.

## When rent is due
Rent is due on the 1st of each month. Payments received after the 5th incur the $75 late
fee. Payment methods are the resident portal, or a check or money order delivered to the
leasing office during office hours. Cash is not accepted.

This assistant cannot see any individual's balance, payment history, or lease charges.
