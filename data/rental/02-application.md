# Maple Court Apartments: Applying

## Application process
Applications are submitted online at the Maple Court resident portal, or on paper at the
leasing office during office hours. Each adult 18 or older who will live in the unit
must submit a separate application.

The application fee is $50 per adult applicant and is non-refundable. A $300 holding
deposit reserves a specific unit for up to 7 days. If the application is approved and
the lease is signed, the holding deposit is applied to the first month's rent. If the
applicant withdraws, the holding deposit is forfeited.

Processing typically takes 2 to 3 business days once all documents are received.

## Required documents
Every applicant must provide:
- A government-issued photo ID.
- Proof of income covering the last 30 days, such as two recent pay stubs, an offer
  letter, or the most recent tax return for self-employed applicants.
- Contact details for the two most recent landlords, if any.

## Screening criteria
Published screening criteria for this property are:
- Gross monthly household income of at least 3 times the monthly rent.
- No unpaid judgment from a previous landlord.
- A credit and background check, run for each adult applicant.

A co-signer is permitted when income is below the 3x threshold. A co-signer must earn at
least 5 times the monthly rent and must complete their own application.

This assistant cannot tell any individual whether they would be approved, check the
status of a submitted application, or see unit availability.
