# Maple Court Apartments: Amenities

## Hours and access
All amenities are opened with the resident key fob. A lost fob costs $25 to replace.

- Fitness center: open daily, 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
- Pool: open seasonally, May 1 through September 30, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. There is no
  lifeguard; residents swim at their own risk.
- Resident lounge and co-working room: open daily, 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. The lounge can
  be reserved for private events through the leasing office for a $75 fee plus a $150
  refundable cleaning deposit.
- Package room: accessible 24 hours with a fob. Packages are held 10 days, then returned
  to the carrier.
- Laundry: each unit has a full-size washer and dryer. There is no shared laundry room.

## Other amenities
A fenced dog run is located behind building C. Outdoor grills are by the pool deck and
are first come first served. Bicycle storage is in the garage; bikes must be registered
with the leasing office. There is no on-site storage unit rental at this property.

## Guests at amenities
Residents may bring up to 2 guests to the pool or fitness center and must accompany them
at all times. Guests under 16 must be accompanied by an adult resident.

## Wi-Fi
Common-area Wi-Fi is provided in the lounge and pool deck. In-unit internet is arranged
by the resident directly with a provider.
