# Maple Court Apartments: Move-In and Move-Out

## Before move-in
Before keys are released, the resident must have:
- A signed lease from every adult occupant.
- The first month's rent and the security deposit paid in full.
- Proof of renters insurance with at least $100,000 in liability coverage, naming Maple
  Court Apartments as an interested party.
- Utilities for electricity transferred into the resident's name effective the move-in
  date.

Keys are released at the leasing office during office hours on or after the lease start
date. Keys are not released outside office hours.

## Move-in inspection
A move-in condition form is provided with the keys. It must be completed and returned
within 5 days. Anything not noted on that form may be treated as resident damage at
move-out.

## Moving hours and elevator
Moving is permitted 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. The service elevator in buildings B and C
can be reserved through the leasing office at no charge. Moving trucks may not block
fire lanes or the trash enclosure.

## Notice and move-out
Written notice to vacate is required at least 60 days before the move-out date, through
the resident portal or in writing at the leasing office.

A pre-move-out walkthrough can be requested in the final 2 weeks. It is optional and
identifies likely deductions in advance.

## Move-out condition
At move-out the unit must be empty, broom clean, with all keys, fobs and parking decals
returned to the leasing office. Carpets must be professionally cleaned, with the receipt
left in the unit or emailed to the office. Nail holes should be patched; full repainting
is not required unless walls were painted a non-standard color without approval.

## Security deposit return
The security deposit, less any deductions, is returned within 30 days of move-out to the
forwarding address on file. An itemized statement of deductions is included. Residents
must provide a forwarding address at move-out.

## Common deductions
Published examples of deductions beyond normal wear:
- Full unit cleaning if left unclean: $200.
- Carpet cleaning if not professionally cleaned: $150.
- Unreturned key, fob or decal: $25 each.
- Wall repair beyond nail holes: billed at actual cost.
- Trash or belongings left behind: $100 minimum, plus disposal cost.

This assistant cannot calculate any individual's deposit return or see any account.
