# Maple Court Apartments — Parking

## Resident parking
Each unit receives 1 unreserved surface parking space at no additional charge. A parking
decal is issued at move-in and must be displayed on the rear windshield. A replacement
decal costs $15.

Reserved covered spaces in the garage are available for $40 per space per month, subject
to availability. The leasing office maintains the reserved-space waitlist; this
assistant cannot check the waitlist or reserve a space.

A unit may hold at most 2 parking spaces total, in any combination of unreserved and
reserved.

## Guest parking
Guest spaces are marked GUEST near the leasing office and are limited to 24 consecutive
hours. Guests staying overnight for more than one night need a temporary pass from the
leasing office.

## Towing
Vehicles are towed at the owner's expense for parking without a decal, parking in a
reserved space not assigned to them, blocking a fire lane or dumpster, or parking in an
accessible space without proper plates or placard.

## Vehicle restrictions
Vehicle repairs and oil changes are not permitted on the property. Inoperable or
unregistered vehicles may not be stored on the property. RVs, boats and trailers are not
permitted.

## Electric vehicles
There are 4 shared EV charging spaces in the garage, available first come first served,
limited to 4 hours per session. Charging is billed at $0.30 per kilowatt-hour through
the charging provider's own app.
