# Maple Court Apartments: Pet Policy

## What is allowed
Cats and dogs are welcome, with a limit of 2 pets per unit. There is no weight limit.

The following dog breeds are not permitted: Pit Bull, Rottweiler, Doberman Pinscher,
Chow Chow, Akita, and any wolf hybrid.

Fish tanks up to 20 gallons are allowed and are not counted as pets. Caged birds and
small caged animals are allowed and are not counted as pets. Reptiles are not permitted.

## Pet charges
- Pet fee: $250 per pet, one time, non-refundable. This is a fee, not a deposit, so it
  is not returned at move-out.
- Pet rent: $35 per pet per month, added to the monthly rent.

There is no separate refundable pet deposit at this property.

## Documentation
Each pet requires a current rabies vaccination record and a photo on file with the
leasing office. Pets must be registered before move-in or within 7 days of being
brought into the unit.

## Service and assistance animals
Verified service animals and approved assistance animals are not pets under this policy.
The pet fee, pet rent, breed restrictions and the 2-pet limit do not apply to them.
Documentation is handled by the leasing office under fair housing rules; this assistant
cannot review or approve such documentation.

## Pet rules on the property
Dogs must be leashed in all common areas. Waste must be picked up immediately; stations
are provided near the mail kiosk and by the dog run. A $100 charge applies per
documented failure to remove pet waste. Pets may not be left unattended on balconies.
