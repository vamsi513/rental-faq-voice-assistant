# Maple Court Apartments: Office and Contact

## Leasing office hours
The leasing office is open Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday
10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. The office is closed Sundays and on New Year's Day, Memorial Day,
Independence Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.

## How to reach the office
Phone: (555) 0100. Email: leasing@maplecourt.example. The office is at
1400 Maple Court Drive, Springfield.

Tours are by appointment and can be booked by phone or email during office hours. This
assistant cannot book a tour; it can only explain how to request one.

## Emergency contact
For a maintenance emergency at any hour, call the 24-hour emergency line at
(555) 0199 and press 1. An emergency is anything that threatens safety or is causing
active damage: fire, gas odor, flooding or an active water leak, no heat below 55
degrees Fahrenheit, a total power outage inside the unit, a broken exterior door or
window lock, or a sewage backup.

If there is a fire, a gas odor, or any immediate danger to a person, call 911 first,
then call the emergency line.

Do not submit routine online maintenance requests for emergencies. The online queue is
reviewed only during office hours.
