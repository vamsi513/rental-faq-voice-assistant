## About this corpus
Every document in data/rental/ describes MAPLE COURT APARTMENTS, a property that does
not exist. Addresses, phone numbers, prices, policies and staff names are invented for a
personal prototype. They describe no real company and apply to no real tenant.

The assistant answers only from these documents. It has no booking system, no tenant
accounts, no unit availability feed and no payment data, so it cannot answer questions
about a specific person's application, balance or lease.
